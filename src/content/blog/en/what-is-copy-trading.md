**Copy trading** means an investor's trades are automatically mirrored into your account by the exchange. At Executor Trading, the one being copied isn't a person but a rule-based algorithmic strategy: **ExecutorBTC**.

## Where does your money sit?

With copy trading you don't send your funds to anyone. The money stays **in your own Binance or Bybit account**; the system only mirrors trade entries and exits into it.

## How does ExecutorBTC trade?

- It trades **BTC/USDT futures only**, usually with **2x leverage**.
- It is a **trend-following** system: it aims to catch large moves while keeping losses small.
- Entries and exits follow fixed rules; there is no manual intervention.
- Because it trades infrequently, the combined impact of fees and funding has stayed below ~5% per year in long-term live use.

## Risks

No strategy is risk-free. Short-term drawdowns happen, leverage can amplify losses, and past performance does not guarantee future results. Only trade with capital you can afford to lose. See the [risk disclosure](/legal/risk/) for details.
