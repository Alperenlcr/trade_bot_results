**ExecutorBTC has had seven versions since January 2025.** v1 launched on 1 January 2025 and v7 on 15 November 2026. The website publishes the results of the two most recent ones, v6 and v7, and links between them.

This post lists every release date, explains what each stage was about, and shows how to read the version marker on the [performance chart](/#performance).

## When was each version released?

| Version | Release date | Main focus |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | 1 January 2025 | First release |
| v2 | 15 May 2025 | Performance improvements |
| v3 | 14 June 2025 | Performance improvements |
| v4 | 29 December 2025 | Performance improvements |
| v5 | 11 March 2026 | Performance improvements; public copy-trade portfolios started |
| v6 | 30 May 2026 | System and security changes; 2x leverage |
| v7 | 15 November 2026 | Performance improvements; leverage raised to 3x |

Each upgrade from v1 to v5 brought performance improvements. v6 was different: it mainly changed the system itself and its security, not the trading results. v7 returns to performance improvements.

## Did the leverage change?

Yes. **v6 trades with 2x leverage; v7 trades with 3x leverage.** The leverage was raised together with the v7 performance improvements. Trades on the [trades table](/#trades) are labelled with the leverage of the version being viewed (for example "Long 2x" on v6 and "Long 3x" on v7).

Higher leverage magnifies gains and losses alike: drawdowns can be deeper and the liquidation risk is higher than with 2x. If you copy the portfolio, check the leverage you have set on your platform and decide whether you want to follow the new 3x level.

## What changed with v5?

v5 is the version with which the public copy-trade portfolios started on Binance and Bybit. Until then ExecutorBTC could not be followed through [copy trading](/blog/what-is-copy-trading/). If you want to know how that works, start with that post.

## Why is v5 not on the website?

v5 and v6 have the same performance. Publishing both would show the same results twice, so the site starts the record at v6.

## Why does the website show only v6 and v7?

The site keeps one clear record per version instead of a long list. The current version, v7, is on the main page and keeps updating. v6 is kept as a frozen archive at [/v6/](/v6/) and will not be updated again; its numbers stop on 15 November 2026, the day v7 took over.

## How do I see where the switch happened?

On the [performance chart](/#performance), a dashed vertical line with a **V** circle marks 15 November 2026. Clicking the circle opens a short note and a button that takes you to the v6 page. The [trade history](/#trades) has the same **V** circle next to the position that was open at the switch.

On the v6 page, a **V** button stays in the corner while you scroll and takes you back to the latest version. The page you land on opens at the same place you were reading.

## Do live portfolios already run v7?

Yes. The live copy-trade accounts have been moved to v7, so new trades there now follow v7. Trades opened under v6 stay in the history, and the v6 page keeps that record. Check the [performance panel](/#performance) for the current picture.

## Risks

A new version does not guarantee better results. Backtests are not a promise of future returns, and copy trading with leverage can lose money. Read the [risk disclosure](/legal/risk/) before you follow any portfolio.
